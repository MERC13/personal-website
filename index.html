Finding a robot’s mistake isn’t fixing it

2026 · robot learning · audit

Can you steer a robot by rewording its instruction?

644 simulated episodes of a frozen π0.5 policy on 8 LIBERO tasks with 74 prompts. Rephrasing barely moves success, a misleading prompt can sink it, and only 2 of 8 tasks had headroom above the original instruction.
